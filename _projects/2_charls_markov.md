**2023 – 2024 · West China Biomedical Big Data Lab** · with Dr. Fashu Xu and Dr. Xiaolei Liu

Using the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS), this project characterized how older adults move between
sarcopenia, obesity, and sarcopenic obesity over time.

- Cleaned and processed 16,000+ annual samples (2011–2015) in Python, yielding ~4,000 valid samples per wave after quality control.
- Fit a continuous-time Markov model in R to estimate transition probabilities over 1-, 2-, and 4-year intervals.
- Built a covariate-adjusted model to assess how socio-demographic factors affect transitions, reporting hazard ratios and 95% CIs.
