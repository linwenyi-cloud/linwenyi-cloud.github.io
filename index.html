Hi, I’m Wenyi (Kellin) Lin, a PhD student in Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISE) at the University of Southern California, where I started in Fall 2025, advised by Shinyi Wu, PhD. Before USC, I earned bachelor’s degrees in Industrial Engineering from the University of Pittsburgh (Minor in Mathematics) and the Sichuan University–Pittsburgh Institute.

My research sits at the intersection of health economics and AI in health care, with a focus on how new technologies can expand access to care for underserved populations. Methodologically, I work with decision tree–Markov models, probabilistic sensitivity analysis, and multi-state models of disease progression.

My current work uses Markov cohort modeling to evaluate the cost-effectiveness of AI-assisted tele-glaucoma screening in the Los Angeles County Department of Health Services safety-net system, comparing it against conventional human-grader tele-screening in a high-risk diabetic population.

Research interests: cost-effectiveness analysis · decision modeling · AI-enabled screening · tele-ophthalmology · health equity
